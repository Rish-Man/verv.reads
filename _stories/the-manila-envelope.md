date: 2026-11-28
teaser: " A secretive late-night handoff in a dimly lit diner comes with high
  stakes—and an even bigger surprise."
---
Joe sat at the back booth of the dimly lit diner, nervously tapping his fingers against a manila envelope. Across from him sat a man in a sharp charcoal suit, checking his gold watch.

"Do you have it?" the suited man asked, his voice barely above a whisper.

Joe slid the envelope across the scarred tabletop. "Every single detail. Dates, names, locations. If this gets out, it’s over for them."

The man opened the flap, peered inside, and let out a slow, satisfied breath. He reached into his jacket pocket, pulled out a thick stack of cash, and pushed it toward Joe. "Pleasure doing business with you. You’re sure nobody followed you here?"

"Positive," Joe said, pocketing the cash and standing up. "Just make sure you keep your end of the bargain."

"Count on it," the man replied, already pulling out his phone to make a call as Joe walked out into the rain.

The man in the suit dialed a number, his eyes scanning the contents of the envelope. When the line connected, he smiled coldly. "It’s done. I have the entire guest list. Now we know exactly who’s coming to Sarah’s surprise birthday party, and we can finally plan a better one."
